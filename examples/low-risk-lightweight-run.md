# Low-risk lightweight run

> Synthetic scenario

## Question

Should a team rename a draft meeting note before sharing it internally?

## Plan

- **Scope:** Internal draft naming only.
- **Complexity budget:** Low; reversible and low consequence.
- **Acceptance criterion:** Choose a clear title that does not imply approval.

## Apply and Qualify

Candidates: “Decision Record,” “Draft Discussion Notes,” and “Final Meeting Notes.”

“Final Meeting Notes” is rejected because the document is not final. “Draft Discussion Notes” accurately communicates scope and status.

## Authority Gate

- **Surviving candidate:** Draft Discussion Notes
- **Status:** Authorized decision
- **Authority:** The document owner may choose a descriptive internal draft title.

## Unify

Rename the document **Draft Discussion Notes**.

- **Falsifier:** If the notes become approved minutes, update the title.
- **Final status:** `DONE`

The receipt is short because the action is routine and reversible.
