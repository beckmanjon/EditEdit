# Mythic-quarantine run

> Synthetic scenario

## Question

Why is a project team repeatedly delaying a decision?

## Apply: Mythic signal

A participant describes the team as “waiting for a perfect map before leaving the harbor.” This is symbolic material. It may illuminate a possible pattern of narrative capture or an assumption that certainty is required.

## Quarantine and translation

The statement is not evidence that fear, indecision, or a specific cause exists. It cannot determine responsibility or authorize action.

Translate it into testable questions:

- What decision criteria are currently missing?
- What would be sufficient evidence for a reversible next step?
- Is the team treating uncertainty as a reason to avoid all action?

## Qualify

Inspect the decision record and missing criteria. If evidence shows that authority or scope is missing, return `NEEDS_CONTEXT` or escalate. If criteria are clear and the action is reversible, propose a bounded next step.

## Unify

Record the symbolic statement as a quarantined prompt, not as evidence.
