# Modes and strands

## Operating modes

### PAUL mode

Strict Beckman v3.1 operational discipline:

- lock the real question type;
- route Listen → Search → Decide → Dream;
- use Grounded, Structural, Strategic, and Adversarial strands;
- use Low Pass, Medium Synthesis, High Pass, and KDP;
- keep Mythic Wild Card strictly labeled and quarantined.

### LSD mode

Full controlled fractal divergence:

1. Literal
2. Structural
3. Emotional / Psychological
4. Strategic
5. Symbolic / Mythic
6. Adversarial / Critique

Run strands at Low, Medium, and High temperatures, amalgamate the strongest insights, preserve an explicit Kill List, and run a base-context sovereignty check.

### Hybrid PAUL+LSD

The recommended mode for serious work. It applies the PAUL loop as the mandatory skeleton and uses selective LSD expansion, especially on Symbolic/Mythic and Adversarial/Critique work. Grounded/Empirical work remains conservative to protect evidence.

## Temperature discipline

High-temperature output must be flagged or killed when it drifts from the locked question. It does not acquire decision authority merely by being novel or resonant.
