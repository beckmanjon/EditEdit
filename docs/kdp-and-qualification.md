# KDP and qualification

KDP is the final decision gate in PAUL mode and Hybrid PAUL+LSD mode. It follows qualification; it does not turn raw resonance, confidence, or attention into authority.

## Execute / Qualify

Inside Apply:

1. Execute bounded research, synthesis, or analysis.
2. Independently qualify outputs against the locked question and acceptance criteria.
3. Surface escalation statuses or diagnostic routing where applicable.

## KDP / final decision gate

The final gate records:

- action now / preferred path;
- invalidation criteria;
- what would change the answer;
- what must be watched next;
- vehicle or capital-efficiency notes when relevant.

Mythic material remains labeled as Symbolic Insight, Bias Warning, or Poetic Color. Poetic Color is excluded from decision logic. Symbolic Insight and Bias Warning may prompt additional qualification, but cannot independently establish facts or authorize action.
